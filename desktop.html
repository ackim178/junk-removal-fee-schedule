The two attached rate sheets aren't landfill tipping fees

RSSN-RATES-2025-2026.pdf and Republic_Services_Rates_Effective_July_1,2025-June_30,2026.pdf are the franchise billing schedules Republic Services / Clark County charges HOAs, businesses, motels, and medical/sewage-waste customers for curbside collection contracts — not the price you pay to self-haul a load to the landfill or transfer station. That self-haul "gate rate" is a separate, publicly posted number (below), and it's the actual cost driver for your business.

Real disposal costs for a self-haul operator in the Las Vegas area

Apex Regional Landfill (the only landfill in Clark County, owned/operated by Republic Services under its 20-year county franchise): posted self-haul gate rate is $37.54/ton, weighed on entry. ~30 miles north of the Strip.

Republic's Cheyenne & Henderson Transfer Stations accept most general items 7 days/week, 7 a.m.–3 p.m. — many household items free, but refrigerant-containing appliances carry a separate fee.

Western Elite (private, non-franchise) runs in-town yards that bill by the cubic yard instead of by weight — worth comparing for smaller loads since they're closer to the Strip.

Fees and accepted-item lists change — call Republic Services at 702-735-5151 or the scale house directly before you lock in a routing plan.

Why labor is in the cost model

Right now the labor rate is 100% your own time (driving, loading, the dump run, unloading) — set it well above your take-home target, not equal to it. It needs to absorb self-employment tax, insurance, equipment wear, and the hours you spend scheduling/invoicing that no single job pays for directly. The helper above walks through that build-up. Keep the breakdown internal — quote customers one flat price per load or item, not an itemized labor/fuel/dump-fee bill.

Towing capacity — verify before you load up

A 2020 Ram 1500 3.6L V6 4x4's max tow rating runs roughly 6,390–7,730 lbs depending on cab/bed/axle-ratio, and max payload (which must include trailer tongue weight) tops out around 2,300 lbs. With a 4,000 lb empty trailer, that leaves roughly 2,400–3,700 lbs of cargo at the truck's absolute limit — the default above (2,800 lbs) already builds in a safety margin for Las Vegas heat, grades, and tongue weight. Check your exact door-jamb Tire & Loading placard and the trailer's own VIN data plate, and don't run at the raw maximum on every job.

Freon and oil rules that affect pricing

Nevada requires refrigerator/freezer doors and latching mechanisms removed before disposal, and the refrigerant must be recovered by a certified tech before the unit can go through normal channels — that's what the Freon handling fee covers. Used motor oil and other liquids must never be poured loose into your trailer; haul them in sealed, upright containers and drop them at an approved recycler (many auto parts stores take oil free) or Republic's household hazardous waste sites in North Las Vegas / Henderson (Wed–Sat, rotating schedule).
