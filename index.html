These two rate sheets aren't landfill tipping fees

RSSN-RATES-2025-2026.pdf and Republic_Services_Rates...pdf are Republic Services / Clark County's franchise billing rates for curbside collection contracts (HOAs, businesses, motels) — not what you pay to self-haul a load to the landfill. That "gate rate" is a separate, publicly posted number below, and it's your real cost driver.

Real disposal costs, Las Vegas area

Apex Regional Landfill — the only landfill in Clark County: self-haul gate rate $37.54/ton, weighed on entry, ~30 miles north of the Strip.

Republic's Cheyenne & Henderson Transfer Stations — most general items free, 7 days/week 7am–3pm; Freon appliances carry a separate fee.

Fees change — call Republic Services at 702-735-5151 before finalizing a routing plan.

Why labor is in the cost model

The labor rate is 100% your own time — set it well above your take-home target, not equal to it. It absorbs self-employment tax, insurance, equipment wear, and non-billable hours. Keep the breakdown internal — quote customers one flat price, not an itemized bill.

Towing capacity — verify before you load up

A 2020 Ram 1500 V6 4x4's max tow rating runs 6,390–7,730 lbs depending on config; max payload tops out around 2,300 lbs. With a 4,000 lb empty trailer, the default above (2,800 lbs) already builds in a safety margin. Check your door-jamb placard and trailer VIN plate to confirm your exact numbers.
